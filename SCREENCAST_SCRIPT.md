# Demo Screencast Script — Slidez AI Try-On
# Shopify App Store Submission (req 4.5.3)

## Requirements from Shopify
- Must be in English (or have English subtitles)
- Must show onboarding start-to-finish
- Must show core features with clear step-by-step narration/captions
- Recommended length: 2–5 minutes
- Format: MP4, 1080p minimum

---

## Setup Before Recording

1. Use a **development store** (not a real merchant store)
2. Make sure the theme app extension is already deployed (`shopify app deploy`)
3. Have at least 2–3 products in the dev store with product images
4. Start with the app **not yet installed** on the dev store (clean install flow)
5. Use a screen recorder with audio (OBS, Loom, QuickTime, etc.)
6. Record at 1920×1080, hide browser bookmarks bar for a clean look

---

## Scene 1 — Install (0:00–0:30)

**What to show:**
1. Open the Shopify App Store listing for Slidez AI Try-On
2. Click **Add app**
3. The Shopify OAuth permission screen appears — show the `read_products` scope
4. Click **Install app**
5. App redirects into the Shopify Admin — onboarding screen loads

**Caption/narration:**
> "Install Slidez AI Try-On directly from the Shopify App Store. The app only
> requests read access to your products — nothing else."

---

## Scene 2 — Onboarding (0:30–1:15)

**What to show:**
1. Onboarding Step 1 — Store type selection (e.g. select "Clothing")
2. Click **Next**
3. Onboarding Step 2 — Plan selection; select the **Free** plan for the demo
4. Click **Get started**
5. App redirects to the main dashboard

**Caption/narration:**
> "The onboarding takes under a minute. Choose your store category and a pricing
> plan — you can upgrade at any time from the Billing page."

---

## Scene 3 — Enable Try-On on a Product (1:15–2:00)

**What to show:**
1. Click **Products** in the left nav
2. Product list appears with toggle switches
3. Find a product (e.g. "Black Dress") — toggle is OFF
4. Click the toggle → it turns ON, badge shows "Try-On Enabled"
5. Optionally open the product settings to show per-product config

**Caption/narration:**
> "Enable virtual try-on per product with a single toggle. No code required."

---

## Scene 4 — Customise the Widget (2:00–2:30)

**What to show:**
1. Click **Settings** in the left nav
2. Change the button text (e.g. type "Try It On")
3. Change the button colour using the colour picker
4. Click **Save settings**
5. Show the confirmation toast

**Caption/narration:**
> "Customise the try-on button text and colour to match your brand — changes
> apply instantly to your storefront."

---

## Scene 5 — Storefront Widget in Action (2:30–3:30)

**What to show:**
1. Open the Online Store → Themes → click **Customize**
2. In the Theme Editor, navigate to a product page
3. Show the **Virtual Try-On Widget** block in the left panel — it is present
4. Exit the Theme Editor
5. Open a product page on the live storefront (Preview mode)
6. The try-on button ("Try It On") is visible below the product images
7. Click the button — the try-on modal/widget opens
8. Upload or select a model photo — the AI applies the garment
9. Show the result

**Caption/narration:**
> "The try-on widget appears automatically on enabled product pages. Shoppers
> click the button, upload a photo, and the AI shows them wearing the product
> — no app download needed."

---

## Scene 6 — Analytics Dashboard (3:30–4:00)

**What to show:**
1. Back in the admin, click **Analytics** in the left nav
2. Show the try-on count, top products, and activity chart
3. Briefly hover over a stat card

**Caption/narration:**
> "The analytics dashboard shows try-on activity across all products so you can
> see which items shoppers engage with most."

---

## Scene 7 — Billing / Plan Upgrade (4:00–4:20)

**What to show:**
1. Click **Billing** in the left nav
2. Show the three paid plans (Growth, Pro, Enterprise) with their features
3. Click **Upgrade** on the Pro plan — Shopify billing confirmation screen appears
4. Do NOT complete the charge (just show it launches correctly), then go back

**Caption/narration:**
> "Plans can be changed at any time from the Billing page — upgrades and
> downgrades go through Shopify's standard billing flow."

---

## Scene 8 — End Card (4:20–4:30)

**What to show:**
- App dashboard with a clean state
- Optionally show the support email: info@slidez.social

**Caption/narration:**
> "Slidez AI Try-On — virtual try-on for clothes, makeup, and accessories,
> built for Shopify."

---

## Checklist Before Uploading

- [ ] Video is in English (or has English subtitles)
- [ ] All 8 scenes are covered
- [ ] No pricing numbers appear on-screen (req 4.2.2)
- [ ] No "boost conversions", "reduce returns", or stat claims appear on-screen (req 4.3.4)
- [ ] No reviews or testimonials on-screen (req 4.3.6)
- [ ] Shopify logo is NOT used in any overlays
- [ ] Video is 1080p MP4
- [ ] Total length is under 5 minutes
- [ ] Submitted in the "Demo video" field in the Partner Dashboard listing editor
